# TikTok 文案 / TikTok copy

[首页 / Home](../README.md)

使用“Hook 类别 → TITLE → CAPTION → Hashtags”。标题负责吸引注意，正文描述可见穿搭并用自然问题收尾。下列为蓝色阔腿裤示例；不是效果或爆款保证。  
Use “Hook angle → TITLE → CAPTION → Hashtags.” Open with curiosity, describe visible styling and end with a natural question. These blue-trouser examples do not guarantee performance.

## 1｜日常搭配 Hook / Everyday styling hook

**TITLE:** Tak tahu nak pakai apa? Try combo biru ni 💙

**CAPTION:** Tudung biru lembut, top krim dan seluar wide-leg denim—simple tapi nampak matching. Korang pilih look ni untuk coffee date atau jalan-jalan?

#OOTDMalaysia #WideLegJeans #DailyOutfit

中文意思：不知道穿什么？试试蓝色搭配。浅蓝头巾、奶油色上衣与阔腿牛仔裤，适合约咖啡还是逛街？  
English meaning: Not sure what to wear? Try this blue combination. Would you wear it for coffee or a casual day out?

## 2｜细节 Hook / Detail hook

**TITLE:** Eh, tali panjang tu yang buat look ni lain 👀

**CAPTION:** Detail tali cerah dengan potongan wide-leg terus jadi fokus. Pair dengan top plain, bagi seluar yang menyerlah. Team tali lepas atau ikat reben?

#DenimStyle #OOTDInspo #FashionMalaysia

中文意思：长抽绳是造型亮点；素色上衣衬托裤子。文案可问偏好，但视频仍保持已选定的抽绳状态。  
English meaning: The long drawstrings stand out against a plain top. Asking about preferences does not mean changing the selected cord state in the video.

## 3｜英文版 / English option

**TITLE:** Your next blue-and-cream outfit idea 💙

**CAPTION:** A soft blue hijab, a cream top and wide-leg denim. The long light drawstrings add the detail. Coffee run or weekend stroll—which one?

#WideLegDenim #ModestOutfit #OutfitIdeas

## 边界 / Boundaries

不虚构价格、库存、折扣、销量、品牌、材质和功效；不把“爆款风格”写成保证爆款。文案独立交付，用户禁止画面文字时不烧录进图片或视频。  
Do not invent prices, stock, discounts, sales, brands, composition or performance. Deliver copy separately when on-screen text is prohibited.
